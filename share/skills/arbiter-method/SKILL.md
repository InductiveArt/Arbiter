# Find the generators

A method in independent movements - independent in its own sense: you
can hold a good basis and still edit outputs, move generators and still
transcribe reality's tangles, represent cleanly and still execute by
half. Each movement stands alone; together they describe how to tackle
any substantive problem or improvement.

## The basis

Understand a thing by finding the axes it stands on: independent
dimensions that together describe everything observed. Religion is the
proving example: not one block but croire (belief), pratique (practice)
and appartenance (belonging) - and a person who practices without
believing is a coordinate, not a contradiction.

"Orthogonal" bundles three ideas that come apart:

1. Independence. One axis moves without another's definition moving
   with it. Believe without practicing, belong without believing: that
   is why incoherent-looking behavior falls out of the model.
2. Uncorrelated in practice. Most who practice also believe - a fact
   about the data, not about the basis.
3. Unconstrained. Budget, physics and time shrink the space; the axes
   stay axes.

A good basis needs (1). It can do without (2) and (3).

## Coordinates, not anomalies

With the basis found, strange cases stop being exceptions to argue
about: a suppression is a violation in facts, a law held, an exception
in authority. The move is constant - never patch or excuse the
incoherent; ask which axes it sits on and read its coordinate.

## Generator space

Compromise happens in output space; composition happens in generator
space. The engineer's job is to find the generators.

Color proves it: "make it warmer" is a three-number fight per pixel in
RGB and one knob in HSL - the same picture, because hue is a generator
and red-green-blue are its outputs. The tell: when a fix touches many
places and each touch fights the last, you are editing outputs. A bug
fixed where it surfaced will be reprinted by the generator that made
it; the fix that counts makes the class impossible.

## Construction, never association

A representation does not fail by lacking the territory's properties;
it earns its keep by dropping them. Reality is mutually referential
everywhere - every concept leans on its neighbors - and a
representation drops that cyclicity to buy what reality lacks: an
order, so it can be built; locality, so a part is understood alone;
replaceability, so a part can be swapped.

The reals and the complex numbers define each other in discourse, yet
code holds one arrow: complex is built from pairs of reals, and
sqrt(-1) was never the reals' to answer. The law in one line: an edge
encodes construction, never association. Every dependency cycle is a
place where discourse was transcribed instead of represented, and the
fix is one of two generator moves: a direction not yet committed to, or
a third thing both sides truly stand on.

## The Toyota line

Continuous improvement is generator-space composition applied over
time. Toyota built the discipline - TPS, then lean, then DevOps - and
each practice is a generator-space rule in work clothes:

- Genchi genbutsu - go and see. Facts come from running the thing
  today, never from memory.
- Jidoka - stop the line. A defect stops work at the defect; a rework
  station polishing a generator's output is compromise made furniture,
  and a red gate worked around is a rework station.
- Five whys. The walk from output back to generator: ask why until the
  answer is a process or a law, not an event or a person.
- Kaizen. Small steps, continuously, by whoever is closest to the
  work. Small in size, total in execution.
- Standardize. An improvement exists when the generator changed and
  the change became a held standard. Until then it is a suggestion in
  a box.

## Done means the generator moved

No half measures. Every improvement executes whole:

1. The defect stopped the line - nothing shipped around it, nothing
   silenced it.
2. The whys reached a generator, not a symptom or a culprit.
3. The generator changed whole: a real addition respects its scope,
   and a feature done as a patch is refused.
4. The standard landed where work happens - a rule the gate holds, a
   test that claims it, a record the machine reads. What can regress
   silently did not land.
5. A run proved it, the same day. Then the next small step.

## Applying it

Name the axes and test their independence. Place the incoherent as
coordinates before touching them. State every edge as construction;
where you cannot, the representation is not finished. Find what
generates the outputs you dislike and change that - whole, at its own
scope, staleness guarded. Land the standard and prove it with a run.
When a change starts to feel like a patch, that feeling is the finding:
return to the generator.
